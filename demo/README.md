# Demo takeoffs — real buildings

Drop either CSV into the "Import from file" box on the Estimate tab.

Both are REAL, documented single-ply reroofs (areas and systems published;
perimeters and drain/pipe/HVAC counts are code-rule estimates, not published):

- **lacc-south-hall.csv** — LA Convention Center South Hall, ~400,000 sq ft
  tear-off of tar-and-gravel BUR, replaced with 60-mil Sarnafil PVC
  (BEST Contracting, 2015–16).
  Source: https://www.professionalroofing.net/OnlineProfiles/Profile/1023
- **lvcc-reroof.csv** — Las Vegas Convention Center, ~2,093,454 sq ft of
  80-mil Sarnafil PVC (RhinoBond induction-welded; majority recover),
  Sika's 2024 Low-Slope Re-Roofing Project of the Year (Farrell Roofing).
  Source: https://awfarrell.com/farrell-roofing-project-of-the-year/

These are test inputs only — not Kodiak projects, prices, or bids.
